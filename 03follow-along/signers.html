Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence played important roles in the founding of the United States.

Thomas Jefferson
Jefferson was a delegate from Virginia and the principal author of the Declaration of Independence.
John Adams
Adams was a delegate from Massachusetts and later became the second president of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
Franklin was a delegate from Pennsylvania and was known as a statesman, diplomat, scientist, and inventor.
