Trading Strategies

Why Use a Trading Strategy?

A trading strategy gives a trader a set of rules for finding and managing trades. Instead of making random decisions, traders can create a plan that explains when they will enter a trade, where they will exit, and how much they are willing to risk.

No strategy guarantees a profit. Market conditions can change, which is why traders should manage risk and avoid risking money they cannot afford to lose.

Common Day Trading Strategies

  • Breakout trading
  • Trend trading
  • Range trading
  • Momentum trading

Building a Trade Plan

Before entering a trade, a trader can identify important price levels and decide what conditions must occur before taking a position. Having rules prepared before a trade can reduce emotional decision-making.

  1. Identify the overall market direction.
  2. Find an area where a possible trade may develop.
  3. Choose an entry point.
  4. Set a stop-loss level.
  5. Choose a profit target.

Practice Before Trading

Beginners can practice strategies with simulated trading before risking real money. This allows a trader to become familiar with the market and evaluate whether a strategy follows their trading plan.

The most important goal for a beginner is not to win every trade. It is to develop consistent habits, protect capital, and continue learning how markets behave.
