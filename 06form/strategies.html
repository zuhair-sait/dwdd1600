01 / THE PURPOSE

Why Strategies Matter

A trading strategy is a collection of rules that explains when a trader may enter or exit a position. The purpose is to reduce impulsive decisions and create a repeatable process.

No trading strategy guarantees profitability. Risk management remains important regardless of the strategy being used.

02 / DIFFERENT APPROACHES

Four Trading Concepts

01

Breakout Trading

Watching for price to move beyond an established support or resistance area.

02

Trend Trading

Studying the direction of price movement and identifying opportunities aligned with that direction.

03

Liquidity Analysis

Examining areas where orders may be concentrated and how price reacts around those levels.

04

Market Structure

Analyzing sequences of highs and lows to better understand market direction.

03 / THE PROCESS

Building a Trade Plan

  1. Review the higher-timeframe market direction.
  2. Mark important price levels.
  3. Identify the conditions needed for an entry.
  4. Determine the maximum risk before entering.
  5. Set an exit plan and record the result.

04 / PRACTICE

Develop Skills Before Risking Money

Simulated trading allows beginners to practice order placement and evaluate strategies without risking actual trading capital.

A trading journal can help identify mistakes, patterns, and areas for improvement.
